Privacy Policy for BR Nearest Station Seat Finder

Last updated: October 2026

🛡️ Our Core Commitment: This extension does not collect, track, sell, or transmit any personal information. All seat queries run locally from your own browser.

1. Introduction

BR Nearest Station Seat Finder ("the Extension", developed by Butterfly Devs) is designed to help Bangladesh Railway train passengers find available seats by checking nearby station alternatives when direct route tickets are sold out on eticket.railway.gov.bd.

We respect your privacy and believe in total transparency. This policy outlines how information is handled when you use the Extension.

2. Information Collection and Handling

The Extension operates strictly on a local-first principle:

  • No Personal Identifiable Information (PII): We do not ask for, capture, or store your name, email, phone number, national ID, or passwords.
  • No Financial / Payment Data: The Extension has zero interaction with payment gateways, bKash, Nagad, cards, or ticketing checkout pages. It only performs read-only seat searches.
  • Search Queries: When you search for trains, the extension temporarily reads the journey details (origin, destination, date, class) directly from your active tab URL to query seat availability. This data remains in temporary memory and is never logged or sent to any external server.
  • Local Preferences: Search configurations (such as max detour distance, number of station hops, or auto-refresh toggles) are stored completely on your local device using Chrome's chrome.storage.local API.

3. Chrome Permissions Usage

The Extension requests the minimum permissions necessary to function:

  • storage: To save your search settings (detour radius, refresh intervals) locally in your browser.
  • notifications: To show a native desktop alert when seats become available during background refresh.
  • https://eticket.railway.gov.bd/*: To inject the nearby station finder interface panel directly onto the official train search results page.
  • https://railspaapi.shohoz.com/*: To query public seat availability data for alternative station pairs via Bangladesh Railway's public ticket API.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, trade, or transfer any user data to any third parties or advertisers. We do not use any third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

5. Security

Because no user data is transferred to external servers, your journey queries stay between your browser and the official ticketing system.

6. Changes to this Policy

We may occasionally update this Privacy Policy to reflect improvements to the tool or changes in legal regulations. Updates will be published on this URL.
